# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in SpecTracer welcoming and respectful
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being patient, constructive, and specific in technical discussion;
- accepting feedback and acknowledging mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users; and
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences.

This code addresses harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and
other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community. When you
see such conduct, report it as described under Enforcement, because it drives
contributors away.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's GitHub
maintainer contact, and send sensitive details privately, because a public issue
exposes the people involved. Reports are
reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action
they consider appropriate to protect the community. Maintainers treat retaliation
against a person who makes a good-faith report as a violation of this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
